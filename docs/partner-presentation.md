# Saputo Toufexis website concept

## The proposition

A focused digital front door for a trial firm whose current website makes visitors work too hard to understand its strengths.

The redesign does not discard the firm's legal library. It separates two jobs:

1. Help a prospective client understand the firm and contact counsel quickly.
2. Preserve the extensive Texas criminal-law library and its existing search value.

## Creative direction: tailored authority

The visual system takes cues from navy tailoring, white shirting, and understated cufflinks:

- Midnight navy communicates seriousness without using flat black.
- White and pale blue-gray create the crisp contrast of a dress shirt.
- Fine rules and subtle vertical stripes reference suiting without becoming decorative.
- Brass and silver details are reserved for important actions and credentials.
- A classical serif gives the firm stature; a clean sans serif keeps legal information readable.

The result should feel prepared, exacting, and personal—not aggressive, theatrical, or templated.

## What the proof of concept demonstrates

- A concise homepage with a clear value proposition.
- Immediate access to a confidential consultation.
- A limited, understandable navigation system.
- A dedicated Insights & Recognition page.
- Reusable layouts for attorneys, practice areas, results, and articles.
- Mobile-first behavior and accessible focus states.
- A WordPress editing experience that does not require a developer for routine copy changes.

## Content principles

- Prove capability with specific results, credentials, and third-party sources.
- Keep each section responsible for one idea.
- Put full legal explanations in the library, not on the homepage.
- Use one primary call to action at a time.
- Date awards and link them to a source.
- Keep case results separate and repeat the required prior-results disclaimer.

## Recommended demonstration flow

1. Open on the homepage hero and explain the tailored visual concept.
2. Compare the new opening screen with the current 4,000-word homepage.
3. Demonstrate the mobile navigation and tap-to-call action.
4. Open Insights & Recognition to show how existing content becomes an asset.
5. Show how a WordPress editor can change copy without altering the design.
6. End on the short consultation path.

## Suggested launch scope

- Home
- Paul Saputo
- Nicholas Toufexis
- Practice Areas
- Results
- Insights & Recognition
- Contact
- Spanish homepage

The existing `/criminal-law/` library and blog remain in WordPress during the first release.

## Success measures

- Consultation starts from the homepage.
- Phone-link taps on mobile.
- Visitors reaching attorney and practice-area pages.
- Reduced homepage depth and abandonment.
- Faster document response and page rendering.
- Search traffic retained across the existing legal library.
