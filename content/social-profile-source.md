## LinkedIn company wording

The public company profile describes Saputo Toufexis as a Dallas criminal-defense firm that fights for the rights of individuals seeking strong criminal defense.

It identifies these services:

- Active investigations
- Criminal trials
- Criminal appeals
- Habeas corpus and post-conviction matters
- Probation violations
- Judicial clemency
- Extradition
- Record clearing
- Sex-offender deregistration

The current company positioning says:

> Saputo Toufexis represents clients facing high-stakes litigation against the government. Led by attorneys Paul Saputo and Nick Toufexis, the firm practices in Texas state and federal courts.

The profile lists the firm as privately held, founded in 2014, and headquartered in Dallas.
